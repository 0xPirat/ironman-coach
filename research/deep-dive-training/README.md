# Deep-Dive Training Research — Index

This folder holds the training-execution layer of the coaching knowledge base, sitting on top of `../ironman-training-methodology.md` (periodization, zones philosophy, PMC, readiness logic) and `../deep-dive-coaching/` (nutrition, recovery, race-day pacing, mental, coaching practice). **workout-library-session-design.md** is the session cookbook — concrete swim/bike/run/strength sets with %FTP/%LTHR/CSS targets, phase variations, and fatigue scaling. **weekly-structure-microcycles.md** assembles those sessions into weeks: phase templates for an 8–14 h age-grouper, sequencing rules, 3:1 vs 2:1 loading blocks, and recovery-week design. **testing-zones-benchmarking.md** is the measurement manual: FTP/LTHR/CSS field-test protocols, zone derivation, retest cadence, and prep-race scheduling across an 18-month build. **injury-prevention-return-protocols.md** covers the big triathlon injuries, ACWR/load rules, prehab routines, pain train/modify/stop thresholds, and graded return after injury and illness (neck check, fever = full stop, COVID staging). **race-preparation-logistics.md** handles everything besides fitness: open-water skill progression, transition training, first-timer gear priorities, Big Day race simulations, race selection, and the final 72 hours. **plan-adaptation-coaching-logic.md** is the core coaching brain: missed-session triage, disrupted-week playbooks, re-planning after 1/2/4+ weeks off, plateau and overtraining diagnosis, adherence tactics, and the weekly-review procedure. Every document ends with an If-Then Rules Summary designed to be applied directly by the AI coach. The `youtube-transcripts/` subfolder holds supporting video transcripts (GTN workout/testing/transition guides, coach interviews on weekly planning, illness return, and first-Ironman lessons) that back and extend these documents.
